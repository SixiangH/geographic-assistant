Why western Europe has a mild climate

The United Kingdom and France lie in the middle latitudes of the Northern Hemisphere. Much of western Europe has an oceanic climate, also called a temperate oceanic climate. It is characterized by relatively mild summers, cool winters, and precipitation throughout the year.

The Atlantic Ocean helps moderate temperature. The North Atlantic Current transports warm water toward Europe, while the prevailing westerlies bring moist air from the ocean. Latitude also affects how much solar energy a region receives.

Mountains can influence local weather. Air rises over the Alps, cools, and may produce precipitation. A rain shadow can form on the sheltered side of a mountain range. These processes are connected to the water cycle, including evaporation and condensation.
